THE LORE

a brief history of questionable pleasure

2010

BORN ON A TABLET

In March 2010, illustrator Matt Oswald draws a bulging-eyed, creepily grinning face for a rage comic about the strange joy of gross things. He posts it. The internet never recovers.

2011

RAGE COMIC ROYALTY

Me Gusta becomes a core cast member of rage comics everywhere — the official reaction to enjoying things you probably shouldn't. Shower water in the ears? Me gusta.

2012

WORLD DOMINATION

T-shirts, masks, keyboards, birthday cakes. The face is everywhere. "Me gusta" — Spanish for "I like it" — becomes the universal caption for questionable pleasure.

NOW

ETERNAL GUSTA

Memes die. Legends don't. A decade later the face still appears exactly when something feels wrong and right at the same time. That's forever content.
